PauseAm - N-ATLaS integration evidence
Ask before you pay. | NAIC 2026 | 3 October 2026

PauseAm uses NCAIR1/NigerianAccentedEnglish for English speech-to-text and NCAIR1/N-ATLaS for constrained relevance checking against reviewed payment-safety guidance. Authenticated model services run on SecretVM; the website backend keeps credentials server-side. Users correct and confirm the transcript before requesting guidance.
