# PauseAm proposed revenue model

3 October 2026. Owner-approved direction, not a launched service, legal approval,
completed recovery, validated price or revenue claim. The owner confirms that a
Nigerian legal partner is available; identity, practising status, engagement and
approval of the specific fee arrangement have not been independently verified.

## Submission wording

PauseAm plans to offer optional human recovery-case support alongside its free
payment-safety companion. With a Nigerian legal partner, we would help clients
organise evidence, prepare complaints and follow lawful bank, eligible CBN,
police and court processes. Revenue would come from a disclosed support fee and
an agreed percentage of money actually recovered, subject to legal and licensing
approval. Recovery is never guaranteed. The service, pricing and demand remain
unvalidated; the current app supplies guidance and user-reviewed report drafts.

## What a client would pay for

| Component | Proposed basis | Status |
|---|---|---|
| PauseAm support | A fixed fee for specifically agreed administrative case work: evidence organisation, chronology, complaint preparation and follow-up coordination | No amount set; written scope and cancellation/refund terms needed |
| Recovery-linked fee | An agreed percentage of funds actually returned to the client, only after receipt is confirmed | No percentage set; legal partner must approve lawfulness, calculation and contract |
| Legal representation | A separate, clearly identified engagement for the legal partner's advice, court documents and representation | Partner availability is owner-reported; verification and engagement pending |
| Third-party expenses | Itemised, authorised and receipted actual costs, such as applicable court filing expenses | No invented universal tariff or payment to obtain influence |

Proposed service revenue is `F + p × R`: `F` is the agreed support fee, `p` the
agreed recovery percentage, and `R` the eligible funds actually received by the
client under the contract. An investigation, frozen balance, court order or
promise of repayment is not receipt. Where `R = 0`, the recovery-linked component
is zero; whether any support fee is payable depends on the disclosed work and
agreed refund terms. Partial recoveries, staged payments, pre-existing refunds,
double billing, taxes and the maximum total charge must be defined before sale.
This is a proposed formula, not a price quotation or forecast.

The percentage is of recovered funds, not a share of the lawyer's professional
fee, as clarified by the owner. Do not implement a referral commission or legal
fee-sharing arrangement without the legal partner's review. Do not call any
PauseAm charge a CBN-approved, police-approved or government-standard recovery
fee: no such approval or universal tariff has been verified.

## Case process and responsibilities

1. Free immediate guidance remains available. A victim can contact their bank
   and use official complaint channels without buying PauseAm support.
2. Human intake establishes the client's instructions, evidence and permission
   to act. PauseAm offers administrative support within an agreed scope. A
   legal practitioner assesses legal remedies and representation separately.
3. Contact the bank promptly. Prepare an eligible CBN complaint about the
   institution's handling when applicable. A regulator complaint is distinct
   from investigating or prosecuting the alleged scammer.
4. Prepare a truthful police/cybercrime report when relevant. The legal partner
   decides whether a court application, preservation step or other remedy is
   warranted and handles the applicable procedure. There is no universal
   bank → police → court → refund sequence and no universal completion date.
5. Log actual correspondence and outcomes, confirm funds returned to the client,
   and invoice only under the agreed terms. Recovery depends on evidence,
   available funds and the decisions of the institutions and authorities.

PauseAm does not impersonate an authority, issue court orders, freeze accounts,
move client funds or guarantee a bank reversal. Proposed operating policy is
that recovered funds go directly to the client, with a separate service invoice.
No paid-service button, checkout, case intake or automated outreach has been
enabled by this proposal.

## Privacy and release boundary

The existing anonymous app is not a legal case-management system. Do not ask for
police reports, identity documents, full bank records or court papers in its
question box, research log, public repository or demo. Before real case intake,
agree a separate secure client/law-firm channel with purpose-specific consent,
access controls, retention/deletion, authorisation and complaint handling.
Never request or store PINs, OTPs, passwords or full account credentials.

Paid N-ATLaS use requires a written commercial licensing arrangement. Its
published scope says commercial use requires separate licensing, and separately
caps use at 1,000 active end users in a rolling 30 days. Model download approval
and the current research pilot do not establish commercial permission. Preserve
the current pilot guard; obtain the relevant permissions before paid launch or
expansion. No paid-model permission has been verified here.

## Evidence and economics to collect

No successful recoveries, revenue or customer willingness-to-pay are evidenced.
The 200+ willing testers remain prospective testers. Validate the proposed
service separately from PS2 usability: need, ability to self-report, affordability,
cost transparency, acceptance of uncertainty and preference for human support.
Measure actual staff hours/costs, counsel costs, case duration, outcome, refunds,
complaints and contribution margin. Forecasts must state assumptions and must not
count money recovered for a client as PauseAm revenue.
