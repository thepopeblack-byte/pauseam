# Amount/date and bank-information update — 3 October 2026

This is source review and engineering evidence, not a participant study or an
independent human content review. No user audio, transcript, private number or
credential is reproduced here. Historical failures remain unchanged.

## Defects addressed

The consumer composer and retrieval guard rejected every digit. This cleared a
corrected transcript or typed question containing an ordinary amount/date. The
text host's stricter digit-free request contract was also incompatible with
passing those values directly. `lib/question-privacy.ts` now permits bounded
currency/payment amounts and calendar date/time forms, while refusing unknown
numeric identifiers and explicit credential disclosures. `lib/text-model.ts`
omits number values before a genuine relevance request. The active ASR retains
its numbers-redacted-v1 policy; numeric leakage still fails at the adapter.
No model container, secret or VM configuration change is needed for this update.

Bank/email/USSD questions were matched to generic scam keywords. The independent
`lib/bank-directory.ts` path now returns source-authored public bank facts with
model=null. This is reviewed directory lookup, not a new N-ATLaS capability.
Missing, multiple or uncovered bank names prompt a bank choice without selecting
an institution automatically. Unreviewed product rates/fees/details abstain.
No contact is extracted from an untrusted message or generated by a model.

## Maintenance and limitations

The directory expires after 17 October 2026 and withholds stale values. A named
team content reviewer must independently confirm every fact before final
submission, repeat official-source checks at least every two weeks, and review
changes immediately when reported. Update the fact, exact source, checked/expiry
dates and this record in a reviewed commit; rerun bank regression tests and
publish. No scheduled scraping or automatic outreach has been configured.

Amounts/dates stay in page state and optional owner-created local report drafts;
normal use retains no questions or transcripts. No research records were added.
Filtering is conservative and cannot identify every disguised credential. The
user is never asked to supply banking secrets or identifiers. Voice numbers are
still hidden by the active ASR; correction may include a non-sensitive amount or
payment date. This update does not claim numeric transcription accuracy.

64 application tests and typecheck passed before publication. The new six tests
cover amount/date use, privacy bypasses, all eight bank fact sets, bank ambiguity,
expiry, unavailable content, injection, unsupported rates, learning boundaries
and omission of numbers in the model request. Authored fixtures are not model
output or human validation. Live publication/request evidence is recorded
separately when completed.

## Combined-question correction

After version 21, the owner reported that single-channel bank questions passed
but asking for a customer-care number and email together returned incomplete
details. The parser previously chose email before evaluating phone intent. The
correction collects all requested channels (email, phone and base USSD menu),
preserves them when a bank must be selected, and keeps ambiguity and expired
sources as abstentions. A new regression exercises seven combined phrasings
for all eight banks. This owner report is a defect finding, not a completed
research interaction or proof of transcription accuracy. The earlier 32/32
version-21 HTTP checks remain unchanged; they missed this combined-request case.
