# Endorsement / registration checklist

PauseAm | Innovation & Enterprise | NAIC 2026

## Required document
The Innovation & Enterprise portal requires a CAC certificate for a registered
entity or a valid government-issued ID for an individual developer.

Portal:
https://ondi.innox.africa/apply/naic/naic-innovation-enterprise

## Prepare the upload
Choose the document that matches the applicant type. Check validity, readable
scans, complete pages and matching applicant or entity names. Confirm the accepted
file type and size shown by the upload control. Use an organisation's certificate
only with its authority.

## Handle privately
Keep the original certificate or ID in private storage. Exclude identity documents
from the public repository, app assets, research exports and demo video. Upload
the required certificate or ID directly to the authenticated portal after checking
the file. This checklist accompanies preparation; the certificate or ID is the
registration evidence.
