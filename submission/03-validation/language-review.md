# Fluent review register — PauseAm

Status: reviewers available according to Kayode; no completed review supplied.

For each English, Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo string, record its key, original source
fact, proposed wording, back-translation, reviewer code, competence/variety,
review date, accept/revise decision and build commit. Require a second review for
“pause payment”, “verify independently”, “keep codes private”, “contact your bank
now” and “recovery is not guaranteed”. Never enter real credentials as examples.

Critical UI states: language choice, recording consent, microphone denied, stop,
discard, upload, transcript correction, uncertain speech, offline, source unavailable,
model unavailable, next steps, source/date, incident export and research consent.

The current interface and source checklists are English. Other ASR language routes
are implemented but unvalidated and do not imply translated safety guidance.
Do not select a language capability checkbox until actual audio, correction,
source-grounded guidance, comprehension and fluent review are demonstrated.
