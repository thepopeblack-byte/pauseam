PauseAm - validation answers for the ONDI draft
3 October 2026 | Reported testing is under way; results pending review

How was the artefact tested with real users, real data, or live benchmarks?

The live English prototype is being tested by users coordinated by our team. As of 3 October, the team lead reports that 25 testers have tried it. We are collecting consented, pseudonymous feedback on device/network conditions, the journey attempted, voice-to-transcript-to-guidance completion, corrections, assistance, action comprehension and defects. Feedback will be reconciled with observer notes and consented anonymous exports where available. Failed attempts and typed-only sessions will remain separate from completed voice interactions. Engineering evidence includes 80 passing application tests and ten final public checks, four of which used the real pinned N-ATLaS text model. These checks are not user sessions. We use fictional payment scenarios and do not request private transaction records or banking secrets. No representative-user success rate or speech-accuracy benchmark has yet been established.

No reviewed participant findings, completion/comprehension percentages, retention, impact or recovery results are available yet.
