PURPOSE AND CONSENT NOTICE
Please take 2-3 minutes to tell us about your experience with PauseAm. Honest feedback, including failures, helps us improve. Participation is optional. Do not include your name, email, phone/account numbers, PINs, OTPs, passwords or real payment records. Google Forms processes your answers. The team will keep individual feedback for up to 90 days and may publish anonymous findings for the NAIC challenge. We do not link answers to your identity, so we may be unable to identify your response after submission. You can leave without submitting.

1. Consent to record this feedback
Required checkbox:
I agree to share this feedback with the PauseAm team and allow anonymous findings to be used in the challenge submission.

2. What did you try, and what happened?
Required paragraph.
Tell us whether you used Check a payment, Report a problem or Learn, and whether you spoke or typed. Include your test date, phone/browser and Wi-Fi/mobile-data connection if you know them. Describe a made-up scenario briefly; do not include a real transaction.

3. Did anything fail or give an incorrect answer?
Optional paragraph.
If something went wrong, describe the step and any error message without private details. Tell us if you had to correct the transcript. If nothing went wrong, say so. This question is optional.

4. What was helpful, confusing or missing?
Required paragraph.
What would you do next after the answer, and why? What is the main improvement you would like? Would you use PauseAm again, and why or why not?

5. What happened in your English voice test?
Required multiple choice; choose one:
- I recorded, checked the transcript and got useful next steps without help.
- I finished the voice journey, but someone had to help me.
- I got a transcript, but no useful next steps.
- I could not record or get a transcript.
- I used text only or did not try voice.

AFTER SUBMISSION
Thank you. Your feedback has been received by the PauseAm team. Please submit once per actual test session. This form does not report a transaction to a bank or CBN.

TEAM OPERATIONS
The user requested removal of the random-code question and evaluator-oriented wording. The form now has five questions including consent, with no names, email fields, tester codes or uploads. It does not collect emails or share response summaries with respondents. Raw feedback stays in the owner's Google Forms account; Google processing is disclosed, and this is not confidential SecretVM storage. Review free text for accidental personal data before exporting or publishing anonymised findings.

Without participant identifiers, form submissions cannot establish a count of distinct people or reliably deduplicate retests. Report response counts as response counts. Distinct participant and documented voice-interaction counts need separate, consented team observations; this form does not prove them. Optional internal research IDs used by an observer are not a required tester form field. Do not infer missing devices, times, trace IDs, consent or successful interactions.

Keep failed attempts and typed-only tests separate from completed voice reports. Feedback is self-reported; objective ASR accuracy, latency and observed comprehension require appropriate independent evidence. No test feedback response was submitted during form editing, and no validation result has been invented.

Delete individual responses and exported copies within the stated 90-day period. The team must carry this out; the form does not automatically enforce it. With no code or identity field, an individual response may not be identifiable for deletion after submission; the consent notice discloses this limitation. Preserve the original setup evidence as historical and use feedback-form-simplified-2026-10-03.json for the revised form.
