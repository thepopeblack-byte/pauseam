PURPOSE AND CONSENT NOTICE
Thank you for testing PauseAm. Please take about 4 minutes to describe what actually happened, including failures. This is English-pilot feedback, not a bank complaint. Do not include your name, email, phone/account numbers, PINs, OTPs, passwords, real payment records, audio or private screenshots. We will use consented, non-identifying answers to improve the app and report anonymous totals in our NAIC submission. Google Forms processes these answers; the team will keep individual feedback for up to 90 days. Keep your random tester code so you can ask the team to delete your response before aggregation. Participation is optional; close this form if you do not consent.

1. Consent to record this feedback
Required checkbox:
I have read the notice above and agree to the team recording my non-identifying feedback and publishing anonymous totals.

2. Your random tester code
Required short answer.
Use the random code provided by the team. If you have none, make a code beginning P- followed by 12 random letters/numbers. Do not use your name, initials, date of birth, phone or email. Keep this code; use the same code if you retest.

3. How was the artefact tested with real users, real data, or live benchmarks?
Required paragraph.
Describe YOUR test, not other people's results. Include: test date (YYYY-MM-DD); student/everyday user/business/other (optional); device and browser; network (Wi-Fi/mobile data/poor signal); journey (Check/Report/Learn); voice or typed; a fictional scenario in a few words; what worked or failed; whether you needed help; whether you corrected the transcript. Say "not measured" if you do not know timings. Do not include an actual payment record.

4. Validation evidence
Optional paragraph.
Provide an app request/reference ID if visible, exact error wording without private details, or which test steps you completed. If you have a consented anonymous evaluation export or redacted screenshot, tell the team privately using your tester code; do not paste private files, financial records, audio or public links here. "No supporting evidence available" is fine. A feedback response alone is a self-report, not an independently observed session.

5. Key results or feedback from validation
Required paragraph.
In your own words: What action would you take after the answer, and why? What was useful? What was confusing, incorrect or missing? Did anything fail? What is the one change you most want? Would you use PauseAm again for a payment concern, and why or why not? Negative feedback is welcome. Do not include private names, transaction details or credentials.

6. What happened in your English voice test?
Required multiple choice; choose one:
- I recorded, reviewed/confirmed the transcript and received usable guidance without help.
- I completed the full voice journey, but needed help.
- A transcript appeared, but I did not receive usable guidance.
- Recording or transcription failed.
- I used text only or did not try voice.

TEAM OPERATIONS
Use the form's Responses tab to review/export results. The form does not collect email addresses, require sign-in for a single response, accept file uploads or share a response summary with respondents. Google still operates the hosting service; do not promise anonymity from Google or confidential VM protection for this form. Keep raw exports private and publish only reviewed non-identifying totals/quotes. Review free text for accidental sensitive information before sharing it further.

Deduplicate by tester code and actual session date. Treat repeat tests separately and preserve failures. A retrospective self-report is not an observed benchmark or a verified completed voice interaction. Pair completed voice reports with consented observer notes or anonymous app exports where available; do not make up missing trace IDs, times, devices or consent.
