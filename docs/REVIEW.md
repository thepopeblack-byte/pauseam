## Review policy

Cards record source section, checked date, review method and expiry. Runtime only retrieves allowlisted, non-expired, source-checked cards. Source checking is an automated editorial review, not human approval. Human sign-off is a launch gate: a qualified reviewer must verify every card, accessibility wording, regional understanding and reporting routes, then record their actual name, date and decision. Never insert a fictional reviewer. Withdraw or revise a card when its source changes. Source links are not fetched during every answer: the app uses a versioned reviewed snapshot. An absent, expired or disabled snapshot produces no answer.

The app is a decision aid, not a bank verification service. It cannot verify a recipient, authorize payment, freeze an account, file a report, reverse a transfer or promise recovery. No risk probability is calculated.
