PauseAm - ONDI validation answers
3 October 2026

How was the artefact tested with real users, real data, or live benchmarks?

Thirty people have tested the live English prototype. We are collecting feedback on the journey used, voice or text input, device/network conditions, transcript corrections, completion, useful next steps and errors. We provide fictional payment scenarios for testing. Our observed-session protocol measures the complete voice journey and whether users understand what to do next. Separately, the current release passed 80 application tests and ten public backend checks; four used the pinned N-ATLaS text model.

Key results or feedback from validation

Feedback from the 30 testers is being collected and has not yet been analysed. Development testing led to fixes for free-form amount/date questions, combined bank-contact requests and generic guidance. The product supports transcript correction, contextual next steps, voice replay/stop, silent typed answers and report-draft download. Four live N-ATLaS text requests completed in 5,960-7,803 ms. We will use participant feedback to assess completion, comprehension and the most useful improvements.
